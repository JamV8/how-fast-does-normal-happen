No. Dice needed 8 and county populations needed 2,450. The extreme-outlier data never got there.

Level 6

What goes wrong with 30 flights

An analyst only gets one sample and builds a “95% range” from it. If that range is honest it misses the true average 5 times in 100. Here are 100 studies of real 2013 New York flights. Count the misses.

 

Over 100,000 runs, the 30-flight range missed 10.2% of the time, almost always by guessing too low. With 235 flights it missed 5.9%.

That’s the Central Limit Theorem, plus the cases where 30 isn’t enough.
